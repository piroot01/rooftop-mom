\section{The mixed antiderivatives \texorpdfstring{$\Gf{p}{m}{n}$}{G}}
\label{app:antiderivatives}

This appendix lists the closed forms~\eqref{eq:Gform} of the antiderivatives~\eqref{eq:G} for $p\in\{-1,1,3\}$,
$m\le3$ and $n\le1$, which are the ones Theorem~\theoRef{touching} needs. Any other one follows from
$\Gf{p}{n}{m}(b,a)=\Gf{p}{m}{n}(a,b)$, and on the axes $\Gf{p}{m}{n}=0$.
